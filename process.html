FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our visa application process

How long does the visa process take?
Processing time depends on the country and visa type. Visit visas typically take 1-3 weeks, while work permits may take 2-4 months depending on the destination country and document verification.
What documents do I need to apply?
Basic documents include a valid passport, photographs, and CNIC. Specific countries require additional documents like police clearance, educational certificates, CV, and bank statements. We provide a complete checklist after your initial application.
How much does it cost?
Costs vary based on country, visa type, and services required. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden fees. Contact us for a detailed quote based on your specific case — initial consultation is free.
Can I track my application status?
Yes! After submission, you receive a unique tracking code. Use the "Check Status" page on our website with your tracking code and CNIC to see real-time status updates anytime, 24/7.
What if my visa is rejected?
We have a 95% success rate due to careful pre-screening. If a rejection happens, we analyze the reason and guide you on reapplication or alternative countries. Refund policies depend on the specific service.
Do you provide accommodation help?
For many work permit destinations (Czechia, Bulgaria, Serbia, Romania), accommodation is provided by the employer. For other countries and visit visas, we help with hotel bookings and accommodation guidance.
